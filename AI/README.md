# AI curriculum: working draft

This folder contains the **not yet presentation-ready** scaffold for the AI
part of the workshop. It is intended to make the current direction visible
without presenting it as a completed curriculum.

## Topic structure

The curriculum is organized around these six topic areas:

| Topic | Guiding question | Activities and notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What is Generative AI? | How does a language model generate text? | [Be the Language Model and LLM Probability Lab](topics/01-what-is-generative-ai.md) |
| 2. Prompt Engineering | How can we describe and test the result we want? | [Prompt Olympics and vibe coding](topics/02-prompt-engineering.md) |
| 3. Fabrications | Why can plausible answers still be wrong? | [Hallucination Treasure Hunt, Fact-Check Race, AI Telephone, and Can AI Know This?](topics/03-fabrications.md) |
| 4. Online Privacy | What information does an AI system actually need? | [Privacy Game and data minimization](topics/04-online-privacy.md) |
| 5. Bias in AI | How can patterns in data affect generated results? | [Bias Laboratory](topics/05-bias-in-ai.md) |
| 6. AI Relationships & Mental Health | What can a chatbot say, know, or feel? | [AI Companion Experiment and Behind the Chat](topics/06-ai-relationships-and-mental-health.md) |

These topics are not yet assigned to fixed days. Colleagues with relevant
training could join at suitable points during the week, particularly for
Online Privacy and AI Relationships & Mental Health.

Activities that connect several topics are collected in
[`cross-topic-activities.md`](cross-topic-activities.md).

## Teaching approach

Students work as **AI investigators**. For each experiment, they record a
prediction, prompt, result, observation, and conclusion. The recurring question
is:

> When should I use AI, when should I verify it, and when should I choose not
> to use it?

This aligns with the Hamburg curriculum: students should experiment with AI
systems and investigate how they work, where they can be used, their limits,
and their effects on society.
