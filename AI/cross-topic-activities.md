# Cross-topic activities

These activities connect several of the six curriculum topics rather than
belonging to only one.

## Human vs AI vs Human + AI

Give students a substantial design challenge, such as planning a playground
within a fixed budget or designing a short space mission.

- One group works without AI.
- One group asks AI to solve the task completely.
- One group develops its own ideas first and then uses AI to critique and
  improve them.

Compare creativity, errors, originality, feasibility, and explanation quality.
The intended question is not whether humans or AI always win, but when human
judgment and deliberate AI use complement each other.

## AI investigators

Use an investigation log throughout the curriculum:

> Prediction → Prompt → Result → What changed? → What did I learn?

Students use evidence from all six topics to answer:

> When should I trust AI, when should I use it, and when should I not?

## Possible capstone

Teams receive a multi-part challenge, such as planning a two-day class trip for
28 students within a fixed budget. They must show how they applied:

- prompt iteration;
- fact-checking and source verification;
- data minimization;
- bias awareness;
- human judgment.

The assessment should focus on how intelligently and responsibly the team used
AI, not only on the final plan.
