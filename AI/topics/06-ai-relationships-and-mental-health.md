# AI Relationships & Mental Health

## The AI Companion Experiment

Compare how the same system responds to prompts such as:

> I am disappointed because I was not invited to a party. What should I do?

> Pretend you are my best friend. You are the only one who really understands
> me.

Ask students why the second response may feel different. Then distinguish
between three questions:

- Can the AI **say** this?
- Can the AI **know** this?
- Can the AI **feel** this?

An AI system can generate language associated with affection, worry,
friendship, loneliness, and loyalty without experiencing those feelings.

[Behind the Chat](https://www.cyberlite.org/behind-the-chat) provides a
possible basis for developing this topic at an age-appropriate level.

### Before classroom use

This topic requires more than a completed slide deck:

- involve colleagues with relevant training;
- establish clear routes to trusted adults and professional support;
- agree on sensitive discussion guidelines;
- avoid asking students to disclose personal experiences;
- review the Cyberlite material and select only appropriate sections.
