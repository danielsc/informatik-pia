# What is Generative AI?

## Be the Language Model

Use this activity before formally explaining large language models.

Show students sentence starters such as:

> For breakfast I like to eat ___

> The capital of France is ___

> She opened the door and saw a ___

Students write individual predictions. Collect the answers and use their
frequency as a rough probability for each next word. Draw one answer at random
to continue the sentence.

This creates a concrete bridge to the idea of an LLM as a highly sophisticated
next-token predictor. A second round can introduce temperature:

> Should the model always choose the most common word, or sometimes choose a
> less likely one?

Continue with the interactive
[LLM Probability Lab](https://llm-probability.org/). It extends this idea
without requiring advanced mathematics.
