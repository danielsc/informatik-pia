# Online Privacy

## The Privacy Game

Give each group a fictional student, such as **Lena, 14**, and cards containing
different kinds of information:

- first name;
- favorite football club;
- home address;
- school;
- password;
- photo;
- birthday;
- favorite song;
- a parent's phone number;
- medical information;
- homework assignment;
- current location;
- an anonymous poem.

Students sort the cards into:

> Okay to share / Think first / Do not share

Then introduce scenarios:

- Lena wants AI to improve her CV.
- Lena wants AI to diagnose a rash.
- Lena wants help writing an email to her teacher.

The answers are not always binary. End with:

> What is the minimum information the AI actually needs?

This introduces the security principle of **data minimization**.
